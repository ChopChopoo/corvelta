Corvelta

Low stack, direct road feel.

Corvelta was started in 2022 by a small group of road racers who wanted a flat that prioritised ground feel over stack height. Every shoe is built around the ThinPulse midsole, a low-density foam tuned for short, fast efforts. Our philosophy is restraint: as little shoe as the runner can use without losing protection. We build for road 5K, 10K, and the occasional half marathon, not for everything. Corvelta ships from a single facility in Vermont and is sold direct to keep the line short and the price honest.
